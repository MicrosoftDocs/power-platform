> [!IMPORTANT]
> Unmanaged developer environments are deprecated, including the environments that Power Platform creates when you sign up for the Power Apps Developer Plan. Developer environments that are [managed environments](../admin/managed-environment-overview.md) aren't affected. To keep your work, enable Managed Environments on your developer environment or move your solutions to another environment before suspension starts. Learn more in [Deprecation of unmanaged developer environments and the Power Apps Developer Plan](../important-changes-coming.md#deprecation-of-unmanaged-developer-environments-and-the-power-apps-developer-plan).
